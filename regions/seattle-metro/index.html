About the Seattle Metro Region

Washington's largest metro area around Puget Sound: Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Everett and dozens of suburbs. Home to Amazon, Microsoft and Boeing, with ferries, mountains and water in every direction.

Counties

King County, Pierce County, Snohomish County, Kitsap County

Highlights

  • Seattle — Washington's largest city: Pike Place Market, Space Needle, waterfront
  • Tacoma — Museum of Glass, Point Defiance Park, Mount Rainier views
  • Bellevue & Redmond — Eastside tech hub, home of Microsoft
  • Everett — Boeing's widebody factory and the Future of Flight tour
  • Bainbridge Island — a 35-minute ferry ride from downtown Seattle
  • Gig Harbor & Poulsbo — waterfront towns on the Kitsap side of the Sound

Living in Seattle Metro

More than half of all Washingtonians live in King, Pierce, Snohomish and Kitsap counties. Seattle and the Eastside (Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland) hold the region's big employers in technology, aerospace, health care and trade, and they also carry the state's highest housing costs. Tacoma, Everett and the south King County cities (Kent, Renton, Auburn, Federal Way) are generally more affordable. Sound Transit Link light rail, regional buses, the Sounder commuter train and Washington State Ferries connect much of the metro, and Kitsap County residents often commute to Seattle by ferry.

Visiting Seattle Metro

Pike Place Market, the Space Needle and Seattle Center, the Seattle waterfront and the Museum of Pop Culture top most first visits. Ferries to Bainbridge Island and Bremerton make easy scenic day trips, and Tacoma has its Museum District and Point Defiance Park. Mount Rainier National Park is about two hours from Seattle, and Snoqualmie Falls and the Cascade passes are under an hour east.
