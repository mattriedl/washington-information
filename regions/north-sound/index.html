North Sound, Washington

Island ferries, tulip fields and the jagged North Cascades.

Islands, tulip fields and the North Cascades — Washington's northwest corner up to the Canadian border.

About the North Sound Region

The northwest corner of Washington between Seattle and Canada: Bellingham, the Skagit Valley tulip fields, Whidbey Island, the San Juan Islands and the gateway to North Cascades National Park.

Counties

Whatcom County, Skagit County, San Juan County, Island County

Highlights

  • Bellingham — Western Washington University, Fairhaven, Mount Baker skiing
  • Mount Vernon — Skagit Valley Tulip Festival every April
  • Anacortes — ferry gateway to the San Juan Islands
  • Friday Harbor — San Juan Island's port town, whale watching
  • Oak Harbor & Coupeville — Whidbey Island, Ebey's Landing
  • La Conner — waterfront arts town in the Skagit Valley

Living in North Sound

Bellingham is the region's largest city, a college town (Western Washington University) with a strong outdoor culture and Mount Baker on the horizon. Mount Vernon and Burlington anchor the Skagit Valley's farm economy, Oak Harbor is home to Naval Air Station Whidbey Island, and Anacortes is the ferry port for the San Juans. Island living means planning around ferry schedules, and housing on the islands is limited and pricey; the valley towns are generally more affordable.

Visiting North Sound

The Skagit Valley Tulip Festival fills the fields with color every April. Ferries from Anacortes reach the San Juan Islands for whale watching, kayaking and Friday Harbor. Chuckanut Drive is one of the state's prettiest short drives, Deception Pass State Park is among the most visited state parks, and the North Cascades Highway (State Route 20) crosses the mountains from late spring to fall.
