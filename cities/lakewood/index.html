About Lakewood

Lakewood is an incorporated city in Pierce County, in Washington’s Seattle Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 63,612. Its population grew by 9% between the 2010 and 2020 censuses, from 58,163 to 63,612. The Census Bureau estimated 62,709 residents in 2024. Lakewood covers about 17.1 square miles of land. By population it is the 20th-largest of Washington’s 281 incorporated cities and towns. The nearest incorporated community is Steilacoom, about 3 miles away. Lakewood was incorporated in 1833.

Lakewood, Washington: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Lakewood, Washington?

Lakewood had 63,612 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 58,163 in 2010).

How big is Lakewood, Washington?

Lakewood covers about 17.1 square miles of land and had 63,612 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 3,720 people per square mile. By population it is Washington's 20th-largest city out of 281.

What county is Lakewood, Washington in?

Lakewood is in Pierce County, in the Seattle Metro region of Washington. The county seat is Tacoma.

How far is Lakewood from Seattle?

Lakewood is about 33 miles from Seattle in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Lakewood, Washington?

The closest incorporated city is Steilacoom, about 3 miles away. Other nearby cities include University Place, Fircrest, DuPont.

What is the ZIP code for Lakewood, Washington?

ZIP codes with a Lakewood mailing address include 98439, 98498, 98499. PO boxes use 98496, 98497. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Lakewood, Washington incorporated?

Lakewood was incorporated as a city in 1833.

What is the cost of living in Lakewood, Washington?

Washington has no personal state income tax, but it has a 6.5% state sales tax plus local sales tax (combined rates of roughly 7.6% to 10.6%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Lakewood is in the Seattle Metro region. See the cost of living section of our Moving to Washington guide.

What school district serves Lakewood, Washington?

Lakewood is served by Clover Park School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Lakewood?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Puget Sound Energy / Tacoma Power (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by Puget Sound Energy, Cascade Natural Gas, Avista or NW Natural depending on the area. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Lakewood.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Lakewood?

Washington gives new residents 30 days to get a Washington driver license, and vehicles should be registered in Washington within 30 days of becoming a resident; you need the Washington license before you can register.
