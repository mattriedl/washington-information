A Leavenworth Christmas means more than half a million free lights strung across a Bavarian-style mountain village, plus four weeks of Santa visits, carolers and Gazebo concerts during the Christmastown festival. For the 2026 season, Christmastown runs Nov. 27 to Dec. 24, and the Village of Lights stays on every day from Thanksgiving through Feb. 28, 2027. This guide covers the dates, the daily schedule, how to get over Stevens Pass, where to park and how to dodge the worst crowds.

Quick answer

  • Christmastown 2026: Nov. 27 – Dec. 24 in downtown Leavenworth, run by the Leavenworth Chamber of Commerce.
  • Village of Lights: 500,000+ lights, free, lit 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily from Nov. 26, 2026, through Feb. 28, 2027.
  • No lighting ceremony: the lights are simply on every day; there is no weekend “flip the switch” moment anymore.
  • Getting there: 117 miles from Seattle on US 2 over Stevens Pass, about 2 hours 15 minutes in good weather.
  • Beat the crowds: go midweek, park free at the Willkommen Park & Ride behind Safeway and ride the free Link bus.
  • Book early: the Reindeer Farm’s holiday tour ($60–$75) is online-only and sells out weeks ahead.

When is Christmastown in Leavenworth?

Christmastown runs from Black Friday, Nov. 27, 2026, through Christmas Eve, Dec. 24, 2026. It is the town’s month-long holiday festival, organized by the Leavenworth Chamber of Commerce, and it takes place in the walkable downtown core along Front Street, centered on Front Street Park, its Gazebo and the Festhalle.

Older guides talk about the “Christmas Lighting Festival,” a few December weekends when crowds gathered to watch the lights switch on together. That format is gone. The Chamber’s December FAQ says the town no longer flips the switch at a set time on weekends; instead the lights stay on every day, which spreads visitors across the whole season.

Most of Christmastown is free; only a few Festhalle evening shows charge admission. Leavenworth had just 2,263 residents at the 2020 Census, so December weekends get crowded.

When are the Leavenworth Christmas lights on?

The Leavenworth Christmas lights are on every day from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m., from Thanksgiving Day (Nov. 26, 2026) through Feb. 28, 2027. The Chamber calls the display the Village of Lights, and it counts more than half a million bulbs on rooflines, balconies and the trees of Front Street Park. Seeing them costs nothing.

That answers a common question: Leavenworth is decorated and lit by Thanksgiving Day, and the display stays up for about three months. It is dark by about 4:30 p.m. in December, so arrive in the afternoon, shop in daylight and stay for the lights.

Because the lights run until the end of February, you don’t have to come in December at all. The Chamber’s Village of Lights guide notes that December brings the biggest crowds, while January and February have more breathing room. January 2027 also has a Winter Karneval with ice carving (Jan. 16–17) and fireworks on Sunday, Jan. 17, at 6 p.m.

A word on the weather: in December 2025, storms, flooding and power outages briefly darkened the lights and closed US 2 through Tumwater Canyon. The display was restored and the highway reopened to two-way traffic on Feb. 10, 2026, according to WSDOT. Winter storms can still disrupt plans, so check conditions before you go.

Leavenworth Reindeer Farm

The farm’s November–December “Celebrate the Season” experience is a guided visit with the reindeer that includes a visit with Santa in the historic barn. Tickets are timed and sold online only, with no walk-ins, and the Chamber suggests booking two to three months ahead for the holidays. If December dates are gone, the farm is open year-round and the regular January–October experience costs less ($32–$60).

Leavenworth Nutcracker Museum

The Nutcracker Museum holds one of the largest nutcracker collections anywhere, and it is a good indoor break when it’s snowing hard. It sits on Front Street, so it fits easily into a walk downtown.

Christmas shopping on Front Street

Leavenworth’s shops lean hard into the season. The best known is Kris Kringl (907 Front St.), a family-run Christmas store since 1986 with ornaments, nutcrackers, village collections, nativities and free ornament personalization. The Festhalle offers free gift wrapping during Christmastown hours.

Sledding, tubing and Nordic skiing

Leavenworth Ski Hill (10701 Ski Hill Dr.), run by the nonprofit Leavenworth Winter Sports Club, has rope tows, the Lt. Michael Adams Tubing Park and about 8 km of Nordic trails, with a 5 km loop lit for night skiing until 10 p.m. It opens only once there’s enough snow, personal sleds and tubes aren’t allowed, and dogs aren’t allowed in winter; check skileavenworth.com for opening dates and prices. For free sledding, the Chamber points families to the Leavenworth Golf Course once the snow is deep enough; bring your own sled.

Sleigh rides

Horse-drawn sleigh rides and snowmobile tours run from outfitters just outside town. They depend on snow cover, so book directly with an operator and ask about their snow policy before you pay.

How do you get to Leavenworth from Seattle in winter?

Most visitors drive 117 miles from Seattle on US 2 over Stevens Pass, which takes about 2 hours 15 minutes each way in good conditions. In winter, plan for more. Stevens Pass tops out at just over 4,000 feet, and snow can bring traction-tire or chain requirements with little notice.

Winter driving rules for Stevens Pass

Before you leave, check the WSDOT Stevens Pass page for conditions, restrictions and webcams. Washington’s signs mean specific things, according to WSDOT’s tires and chains guide:

  • Traction tires required: passenger vehicles need approved traction tires (at least 1/8 inch of tread, marked M+S, All Season or with the mountain-snowflake symbol). Vehicles over 10,000 pounds need chains.
  • Chains required: all vehicles need chains except all-wheel and four-wheel drive, which must still carry chains.
  • Chains required on all vehicles: this includes AWD and 4WD.

Ignoring a posted requirement can bring a $500 ticket. Studded tires are legal in Washington from Nov. 1 through March 31. The Chamber’s day-trip guide suggests leaving Seattle by 7 or 8 a.m. and heading home by about 5 or 6 p.m. so you don’t cross the pass late at night. For a calmer drive, spend the night instead.

Is there a Leavenworth Christmas train?

There is no special Christmas excursion train listed for the 2026 season, but Amtrak’s daily Empire Builder stops at Leavenworth, so you can still arrive by rail. The old “Christmas Snow Train” day trips from Seattle that many people remember are no longer advertised, and the Chamber’s Christmastown and day-trip pages don’t mention one.

According to Amtrak’s current timetable, eastbound Train 8 leaves Seattle at 4:55 p.m. (with stops in Edmonds and Everett) and arrives in Leavenworth at 8:17 p.m. Westbound Train 7 leaves Leavenworth at 7:18 a.m. and reaches Seattle at 11:29 a.m. That timing only works with at least one night in town, but it is a scenic, snow-free way to cross the Cascades. Leavenworth’s Icicle Station (11645 North Road) is an unstaffed platform about a mile from downtown, so arrange a taxi or shuttle ahead. Check Amtrak for current times before you book.

For a day trip without driving, Amtrak’s Thruway bus (operated by Northwestern Trailways) is listed leaving Seattle at 8:50 a.m. and arriving in Leavenworth at 12:10 p.m., with a return leaving at 2:55 p.m.

Where do you park for Leavenworth Christmas?

The easiest option is the free Willkommen Park & Ride behind Safeway (200 Ward Strasse), where the free Link Transit bus runs into town from about 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., seven days a week. A second free park-and-ride is at 990 US 2. Both are stops on Link’s zero-fare Route 32 Leavenworth Local.

Downtown lots are paid year-round, including holidays; you can pay at a kiosk or with the ParkMobile app, according to the City of Leavenworth. The city’s lots include Aasgard, Blewett, Colchuck, Dragontail, Glacier and Horseshoe Lake. The normally free Enchantments and Freund lots become paid parking on weekends from Thanksgiving through December to raise money for local causes. City lots are open 7 a.m. to 3 a.m., with no overnight parking unless posted, and street parking is banned from 3 to 7 a.m. from Nov. 15 to March 15.

On Saturdays during Christmastown, the Chamber says traffic teams work from noon to 8 p.m., and weekend lots fill fast. If you arrive after noon on a December Saturday, go straight to the park-and-ride.

When should you go, and where should you stay?

The best time to visit Leavenworth for Christmas is a weekday (Monday to Thursday) in early December, or any time in January if you mostly want the lights. Weekends from Thanksgiving through Christmas are the busiest and most expensive days of the year, with longer restaurant waits and full parking lots. Midweek you lose the Gazebo music, but you gain Santa time, carolers in the evening and far easier parking.

Lodging in town sells out first on December weekends, so book as soon as you know your dates. If Leavenworth is full or too pricey, look at Cashmere, about 11 miles east, or Wenatchee, a larger city about half an hour down the valley in Chelan County with more hotels. Link Transit buses connect Wenatchee and Leavenworth, and Pangborn Memorial Airport in East Wenatchee is about 30 minutes away.

Tips for a smoother trip:

  • Arrive mid-afternoon on a weekday, shop and eat early, and walk the lights after dark.
  • Make dinner reservations where restaurants take them; weekend waits can be long.
  • Pack chains or confirm your tires qualify, even with AWD.

What is the weather like in Leavenworth at Christmas?

Leavenworth is cold and often snowy at Christmas, with average December highs in the low 30s °F and lows around 20 °F. NOAA’s 1991–2020 climate normals for the Leavenworth station show about 32 inches of snow in an average December, though some years bring much less. The town sits at about 1,170 feet on the dry, east side of the Cascades, so it is colder and snowier than Seattle but sunnier than the west side.

The Chamber’s winter guide calls January and February the most reliable snow months. If a snowy scene is your priority, a January trip gives you the same lights with deeper snow and smaller crowds. Dress for standing still in the cold: insulated boots with grip, a warm hat, gloves and a waterproof layer. Sidewalks can be icy. FAQ

When does Leavenworth decorate for Christmas?

Leavenworth is decorated in time for the Village of Lights to turn on for the season on Thanksgiving Day. For 2026 the lights are on from Nov. 26 through Feb. 28, 2027, daily from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m.

Is Leavenworth Christmastown free?

Yes. Walking the lights, Gazebo music, Santa visits, story time and Festhalle crafts are free. You pay for parking downtown, food, and a few ticketed Festhalle evening shows ($5–$15).

What is the best day to visit Leavenworth for Christmas?

A Monday through Thursday in early December. Parking is easier and lines are shorter, and you still get Santa, carolers (5–7 p.m., Dec. 1–23) and the full light display. Fridays and weekends add Gazebo music but bring the biggest crowds.

Is there still a Christmas lighting ceremony in Leavenworth?

No. The town no longer switches the lights on at a set time on weekends. The lights are simply on every day of the season, so you can visit any day you like.

Is Leavenworth open on Christmas Day?

The lights are on and the streets are open to walk on Christmas Day, but Christmastown events end on Dec. 24, and many shops and restaurants close or shorten hours on Dec. 25. Call ahead before planning a meal.

Do you need chains to drive to Leavenworth in December?

Sometimes. WSDOT can require traction tires or chains on Stevens Pass during storms, and even AWD vehicles must carry chains when chains are required. Check the WSDOT Stevens Pass page right before you leave.

How long are the Leavenworth Christmas lights up?

About three months. The lights stay on through the end of February, long after Christmastown ends on Christmas Eve.
