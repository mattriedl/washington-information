Southwest Washington, Washington

The capital on Puget Sound, the Columbia River and the open crater of Mount St. Helens.

Olympia, Vancouver and Mount St. Helens — the capital region and the Columbia River country.

About the Southwest Washington Region

The state capital Olympia, fast-growing Vancouver across the Columbia from Portland, Longview, Centralia and the country around Mount St. Helens and the Columbia River Gorge.

Counties

Thurston County, Lewis County, Cowlitz County, Clark County, Wahkiakum County, Skamania County

Highlights

  • Olympia — the state capital, Capitol Campus and farmers market
  • Vancouver — Fort Vancouver and a booming waterfront
  • Camas & Washougal — gateway to the Columbia River Gorge
  • Longview — planned 1920s city on the Columbia
  • Centralia — historic downtown midway between Seattle and Portland
  • Stevenson — Gorge town near Beacon Rock and the Bridge of the Gods

Living in Southwest Washington

Olympia, Lacey and Tumwater form the capital metro, where state government is the largest employer. Vancouver, Camas, Battle Ground and Ridgefield in Clark County are among the fastest-growing cities in the state, partly because residents can work in Portland while living in a state with no income tax (Washington still taxes income earned there). Longview, Kelso and Centralia are mid-sized river and rail towns with lower housing costs.

Visiting Southwest Washington

Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument, with the Johnston Ridge area and Ape Cave lava tube, is the region's headline sight. The Washington side of the Columbia River Gorge has Beacon Rock and Dog Mountain, Fort Vancouver National Historic Site recalls the fur trade, and Olympia's Capitol Campus and waterfront make an easy stop on I-5.
