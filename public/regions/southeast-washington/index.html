Southeast Washington, Washington

River country, vineyards and the Blue Mountains on the Idaho line.

The Tri-Cities and Walla Walla — rivers, wine country and the Blue Mountains.

About the Southeast Washington Region

The Tri-Cities of Kennewick, Pasco and Richland where the Snake and Yakima rivers meet the Columbia, Walla Walla's wine country, and the Blue Mountains down to Clarkston on the Idaho border.

Counties

Benton County, Franklin County, Walla Walla County, Columbia County, Garfield County, Asotin County

Highlights

  • Kennewick — the largest of the Tri-Cities, Columbia Park
  • Richland — Hanford history and Pacific Northwest National Laboratory
  • Pasco — farmers market and the region's main airport
  • Walla Walla — wine country, Whitman College, historic downtown
  • Prosser — Yakima Valley wineries and the Walter Clore Wine Center
  • Clarkston — Snake River gateway to Hells Canyon

Living in Southeast Washington

Kennewick, Pasco and Richland form one of the state's fastest-growing metro areas, with jobs at the Hanford cleanup, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, agriculture and food processing. Summers are hot and winters are short and cold. Walla Walla is a historic small city with Whitman College and a large wine industry, and Clarkston faces Lewiston, Idaho, across the Snake River. Housing is typically far cheaper than the Puget Sound region.

Visiting Southeast Washington

Walla Walla has well over a hundred wineries and tasting rooms, and the Tri-Cities add their own wine routes, river trails and boating. The Manhattan Project National Historical Park offers tours of Hanford's B Reactor, Whitman Mission National Historic Site sits near Walla Walla, and Clarkston is a starting point for jet boat trips into Hells Canyon.
