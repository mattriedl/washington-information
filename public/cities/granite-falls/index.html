About Granite Falls

Granite Falls is an incorporated city in Snohomish County, in Washington’s Seattle Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 4,450. Its population grew by 32% between the 2010 and 2020 censuses, from 3,364 to 4,450. The Census Bureau estimated 4,702 residents in 2024. Granite Falls covers about 2.19 square miles of land. The nearest incorporated community is Lake Stevens, about 8 miles away.

Granite Falls, Washington: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Granite Falls, Washington?

Granite Falls had 4,450 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 3,364 in 2010).

How big is Granite Falls, Washington?

Granite Falls covers about 2.19 square miles of land and had 4,450 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 2,032 people per square mile.

What county is Granite Falls, Washington in?

Granite Falls is in Snohomish County, in the Seattle Metro region of Washington. The county seat is Everett.

How far is Granite Falls from Seattle?

Granite Falls is about 37 miles from Seattle in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Granite Falls, Washington?

The closest incorporated city is Lake Stevens, about 8 miles away. Other nearby cities include Marysville, Arlington, Snohomish.

What is the ZIP code for Granite Falls, Washington?

Granite Falls's ZIP code is 98252. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

What is the cost of living in Granite Falls, Washington?

Washington has no personal state income tax, but it has a 6.5% state sales tax plus local sales tax (combined rates of roughly 7.6% to 10.6%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Granite Falls is in the Seattle Metro region. See the cost of living section of our Moving to Washington guide.

What school district serves Granite Falls, Washington?

Granite Falls is served by Granite Falls School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Granite Falls?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Snohomish County PUD (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by Puget Sound Energy, Cascade Natural Gas, Avista or NW Natural depending on the area. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Granite Falls.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Granite Falls?

Washington gives new residents 30 days to get a Washington driver license, and vehicles should be registered in Washington within 30 days of becoming a resident; you need the Washington license before you can register.
