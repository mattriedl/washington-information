About Roy

Roy is an incorporated city in Pierce County, in Washington’s Seattle Metro region, with a 2020 Census population of 816. Its population grew by 3% between the 2010 and 2020 censuses, from 793 to 816. The Census Bureau estimated 803 residents in 2024. Roy covers about 0.48 square miles of land. The nearest incorporated community is Yelm, about 6 miles away.

Roy, Washington: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Roy, Washington?

Roy had 816 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 793 in 2010).

How big is Roy, Washington?

Roy covers about 0.48 square miles of land and had 816 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 1,700 people per square mile.

What county is Roy, Washington in?

Roy is in Pierce County, in the Seattle Metro region of Washington. The county seat is Tacoma.

How far is Roy from Seattle?

Roy is about 44 miles from Seattle in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Roy, Washington?

The closest incorporated city is Yelm, about 6 miles away. Other nearby cities include DuPont, Rainier, Lakewood.

What is the ZIP code for Roy, Washington?

Roy's ZIP code is 98580. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

What is the cost of living in Roy, Washington?

Washington has no personal state income tax, but it has a 6.5% state sales tax plus local sales tax (combined rates of roughly 7.6% to 10.6%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Roy is in the Seattle Metro region. See the cost of living section of our Moving to Washington guide.

What school district serves Roy, Washington?

Roy is served by Bethel School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Roy?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Puget Sound Energy / Tacoma Power (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by Puget Sound Energy, Cascade Natural Gas, Avista or NW Natural depending on the area. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Roy.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Roy?

Washington gives new residents 30 days to get a Washington driver license, and vehicles should be registered in Washington within 30 days of becoming a resident; you need the Washington license before you can register.
