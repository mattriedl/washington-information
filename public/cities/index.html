List of Cities in Washington (A–Z)

All 281 incorporated cities and towns in Washington, with county and 2020 Census population.

Home › Washington Cities

Washington has 281 incorporated municipalities — 211 cities and 70 towns — spread across its 39 counties. Together they are home to about 5.0 million people — about 65% of the state’s 7.71 million residents (2020 Census); the rest live in unincorporated areas. Seattle is the largest city, Olympia is the state capital, and Krupp is the smallest, with 49 residents at the 2020 Census. Every city below links to its own guide with population history, schools, utilities, weather, nearby cities and official links. You can also browse cities by county or by region.
