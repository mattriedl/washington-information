About Ritzville

Ritzville is an incorporated city in Adams County, in the Eastern Washington region, with a 2020 Census population of 1,767. Its population grew by 6% between the 2010 and 2020 censuses, from 1,673 to 1,767. The Census Bureau estimated 1,709 residents in 2024. Ritzville covers about 1.75 square miles of land. It is the county seat of Adams County. The nearest incorporated community is Lind, about 15 miles away. Ritzville was incorporated in 1880.

Ritzville, Washington: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the population of Ritzville, Washington?

Ritzville had 1,767 residents at the 2020 U.S. Census (up from 1,673 in 2010).

How big is Ritzville, Washington?

Ritzville covers about 1.75 square miles of land and had 1,767 residents at the 2020 Census, a density of about 1,010 people per square mile.

What county is Ritzville, Washington in?

Ritzville is in Adams County, in the Eastern Washington region of Washington. It is the county seat of Adams County.

How far is Ritzville from Seattle?

Ritzville is about 189 miles from Seattle in a straight line; the driving distance is longer and depends on your route.

What cities are near Ritzville, Washington?

The closest incorporated city is Lind, about 15 miles away. Other nearby cities include Odessa, Sprague, Lamont.

What is the ZIP code for Ritzville, Washington?

Ritzville's ZIP code is 99169. Some addresses near the city limits use a neighboring town's ZIP code, so check a specific address with USPS.

When was Ritzville, Washington incorporated?

Ritzville was incorporated as a city in 1880.

What is the cost of living in Ritzville, Washington?

Washington has no personal state income tax, but it has a 6.5% state sales tax plus local sales tax (combined rates of roughly 7.6% to 10.6%), and housing is the biggest cost difference between communities. Ritzville is in the Eastern Washington region. See the cost of living section of our Moving to Washington guide.

What school district serves Ritzville, Washington?

Ritzville is served by Lind School District, Ritzville School District. The exact school a student attends depends on the home address, so confirm with the district before you buy or rent.

How do I set up utilities when moving to Ritzville?

Electricity in this area is generally provided by Avista / Big Bend Electric Cooperative (confirm by address); natural gas, where available, by Puget Sound Energy, Cascade Natural Gas, Avista or NW Natural depending on the area. Water, sewer and trash are typically arranged through the City of Ritzville.

How soon must I register my car after moving to Ritzville?

Washington gives new residents 30 days to get a Washington driver license, and vehicles should be registered in Washington within 30 days of becoming a resident; you need the Washington license before you can register.
