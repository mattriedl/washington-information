About Benton County

Benton County had 206,873 residents at the 2020 Census and an estimated 218,190 in 2024. The county seat is Prosser. It covers about 1,700 square miles of land. The county was established in 1905 and is named for Thomas Hart Benton. It has 5 incorporated cities and towns; the largest is Kennewick.
