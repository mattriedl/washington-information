What Washington Information is

Washington Information is an independent, free guide to the State of Washington. It brings together practical facts about all 281 incorporated cities and towns and all 39 counties — population, history, location, official websites — along with step-by-step guides for people moving to Washington and people visiting.

Who it is for

Newcomers comparing places to live, visitors planning a trip, and Washingtonians who want a quick, plain-language starting point before contacting a city, county or state office.

Where the information comes from

  • Population figures: U.S. Census Bureau (2010 and 2020 decennial census).
  • City and county background, incorporation dates, coordinates and photos: Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, used under their Creative Commons licenses with attribution on each page.
  • Government services and programs: official State of Washington websites, linked directly.

We check sources carefully, but details such as utility providers, school assignments and office hours change. Always confirm with the official agency before making decisions.

Independence

Washington Information is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the State of Washington or any city or county government.

Corrections and suggestions
