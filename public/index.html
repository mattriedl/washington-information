Washington Quick Facts: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the capital of Washington?

Olympia is the capital of Washington. Seattle is the largest city.

How many cities and counties are in Washington?

Washington has 281 incorporated municipalities (211 cities and 70 towns) and 39 counties. This site groups them into seven regions: Seattle Metro, the North Sound, the Olympic Peninsula, Southwest Washington, Central Washington, Eastern Washington and Southeast Washington.

What is the population of Washington?

Washington had 7,705,281 residents at the 2020 Census, making it the 13th most populous state. About 2.27 million people live in King County alone.

Does Washington have a state income tax?

Not on wages. Washington does not tax paychecks or ordinary income today. A 9.9% tax on personal income above $1 million was signed in 2026 and is scheduled to begin in 2028, but it is being challenged in court. The state relies on a 6.5% state sales tax plus local sales taxes, property taxes and a business and occupation (B&O) tax, and it taxes large long-term capital gains. See our moving guide for details.

What is Washington known for?

Washington is known as the Evergreen State for its forests. It is home to Mount Rainier and three national parks, Puget Sound and its ferries, the apple and hop harvests east of the Cascades, a large wine industry and companies such as Microsoft, Amazon, Boeing and Starbucks.

What time zone is Washington in?

All of Washington is in the Pacific Time Zone and observes daylight saving time.
